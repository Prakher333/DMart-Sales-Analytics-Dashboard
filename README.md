# 📌 Project Overview  

The D Mart Sales Analytics Dashboard is a Business Intelligence and Data Analytics project focused on analyzing retail sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, regional demand trends, and e-commerce platform contribution through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking.  

This project transforms raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights to support data-driven decision-making and business growth strategies.

---

# 🎯 Key Objectives  

- Analyze monthly sales and order trends  
- Identify top-performing states and regions  
- Study customer purchasing behavior and demographics  
- Evaluate order fulfillment efficiency  
- Compare platform-wise revenue contribution  
- Generate strategic business insights and recommendations  

---

# 📊 Dashboard Insights  

## ✅ Customer Insights
- Women customers contributed nearly **64%** of total revenue.
- Adult female customers formed the largest customer segment.

## ✅ Sales Insights
- Higher sales performance was observed during the initial months of the year.
- Seasonal fluctuations affected sales trends in later months.

## ✅ Operational Insights
- Approximately **92%** of orders were successfully delivered, indicating strong operational efficiency.

## ✅ Regional Insights
- Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and West Bengal emerged as top-performing states.

## ✅ Platform Insights
- Amazon generated the highest revenue contribution among all e-commerce platforms.

# 💡 Business Recommendations  

- Increase women-focused marketing campaigns and loyalty programs  
- Optimize inventory for high-demand categories like Kurtas and Sarees  
- Expand targeted promotions in underperforming regions
